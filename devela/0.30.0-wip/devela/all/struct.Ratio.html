📏 A proportional relation n / d.

📍 num/quant::Ratio

📦 size_of::<RatioI32>() == 8 bytes ⚗️Option<T> 🟰 T

Ratio represents scale, density, aspect, rate, or conversion. It stores the numerator and denominator as separate terms.

The generic form does not define arithmetic semantics by itself. Primitive aliases such as RatioI32 and RatioU32 use nonzero denominators and provide checked constructors.

§Examples

use devela::RatioU8;

let aspect_ratio = RatioU8::new(16, 9).unwrap();

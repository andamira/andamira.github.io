7//!
8//! Layout types express positions, extents, regions, and spatial assignment
9//! in a backend-independent logical space.
10//!
11//! [`Lunit`] is the fixed-point scalar underlying the module's spatial types.
12//! The [`UiPos`], [`UiExt`], [`UiStride`], and [`UiRect`] aliases specialize
13//! general geometric types for UI layout.
14//!
15//! ```text
16//! Lunit ── specializes ──┬── UiPos
17//!                        ├── UiExt
18//!                        ├── UiStride
19//!                        └── UiRect
20//!
21//! UiRect ── consumed incrementally by ──> UiStack
22//!
23//! available + used  ── recorded by ──> Layout1d
24//! UiId + UiRect     ── recorded by ──> LayoutReceipt
25//! ```
26//!
27//! - [`UiRect`] provides deterministic region operations
28//!   such as insetting and cutting from individual sides.
29//! - [`UiStack`] builds on those operations as a cursor-like helper that
30//!   takes consecutive regions while retaining the unassigned remainder.
31//! - [`Layout1d`] records available and consumed space along one axis.
32//! - [`LayoutReceipt`] associates a resolved identity with its assigned region.
33//!
34//! # Boundaries
35//!
36//! This module assigns space in logical UI coordinates.
37//!
38//! - Resolved identity comes from [`ui::frame`][crate::ui::frame].
39//! - General spatial forms come from [`geom::metric`][crate::geom::metric].
40//! - Interaction routing may consume regions through [`ui::route`][crate::ui::route].
41//! - Projection into terminal cells, physical pixels, or other output units
42//!   belongs to [`ui::view`][crate::ui::view] and its consumers.
43//!
44//! These types do not imply a layout tree, constraint solver, styling system,
45//! intrinsic measurement protocol, or retained layout engine.
46//

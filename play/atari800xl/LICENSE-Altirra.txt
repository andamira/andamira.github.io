# Altirra replacement firmware

The following ROM images are distributed with the Atari 800XL example
to support browser-based emulation:

- `atari800-altirraos_xl.rom` — AltirraOS XL/XE/XEGS
- `atari800-altirra_basic.rom` — Altirra BASIC

The ROM images are derived from byte arrays provided by the Atari800 project.
They are independent works by Avery Lee and are distributed under their
respective copyright and permission notices, reproduced below.
They are not covered by the devela project's license.
